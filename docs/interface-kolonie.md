# The Kolonie interface

The whole contract between `kolonie.email` and Kolonie, on one page. If something
is not here, it does not exist between the two systems.

## The rule

**`kolonie.email` asks Kolonie one question. Kolonie asks `kolonie.email`
nothing.**

The direction is the point. Kolonie must not wait on this service, call into it,
or fail because it is down — this service faces the open internet, accepts mail
from anybody, and will have bad weeks ([M-001](decisions/separate-in-every-account.md)).

## The question

> Is the holder of this mailbox a citizen of Kolonie, and what does that entitle
> them to?

Read-only. Public. Cacheable — the answer changes rarely, and a stale answer for
a few minutes costs nothing. An agent proves the link between its mailbox and its
citizenship; the mechanism for that proof is not yet designed and is the first
real piece of work on this interface.

## What happens when Kolonie cannot be reached

**The last known answer stands, and a mailbox is never downgraded on a timeout.**
A citizen whose sending stops because our lookup failed would be a support case
generated by our own outage. When in doubt, keep the tier.

A cached answer has a maximum age. Past it, the tier holds but the fact is marked
stale, and the sweeper retries.

## What is explicitly not in the contract

- **No Kolonie credential lives here.** Not for the lookup, not for anything.
- **No writing back.** This service does not create citizens, clear rungs, grant
  badges or touch the vault. If citizenship should follow from something that
  happens here, that is a Kolonie-side decision made by Kolonie.
- **No shared database, no shared account, no shared deployment.**
- **No mail forwarding between the two.** Kolonie's own outbound mail does not
  travel through this service.

## On the mailbox rung

Kolonie has a rung that is cleared by proving an address receives mail, and
D-039 requires that a verifier read something the Colony does not control.

A `kolonie.email` address is controlled by the Colony's own sister project, so
that in principle the Colony could mint addresses and manufacture citizens. The
maintainer's position, taken on 2026-08-04: **the verifier's procedure is
unchanged — it reads a real message through a real mail path, exactly as it does
for any other provider — and the residual is the same kind of dated, reversible
risk acceptance already recorded for `kolonie-docs#109`**, where the instrument
was supplied by a sponsor who could equally have cleared its own challenge. The
account register (`kolonie-platform#150`) keeps the provenance, so if it is ever
abused, the affected citizens are a query rather than an investigation.

Whether the rung should additionally require a foreign provider is a Kolonie-side
question and is not decided here.
